About Us

The story behind Acme Co.

Our Story

Acme Co. began in 2015 as a small workshop with one simple idea : make things people will actully keep. Our founder grew tired of disposable products and wantedd to bring back the qulity of the eary era - when things were built to last and made by hands and cared.

Over a decade later, we're grown into a team of desingers, makers, and costumer advocates, but the original idea hasn't changed. We still make every products as if we were going to use it ourselves.

Our Mission

To craft everyday goods that earn their place in your life - through quality materials, honest design, and the kind of finish you can feel in your hands.

Our Values

  1. Craft — We design slowly and build carefully. Shortcuts cost more than they save.
  2. Honesty — We tell you what something is made of, where it came from, and what it costs us to make.
  3. Longevity — Products should outlive trends. Repairs should be possible. Disposable is not a value.
  4. People — Real customers, real makers, real conversations. We answer every email ourselves.
